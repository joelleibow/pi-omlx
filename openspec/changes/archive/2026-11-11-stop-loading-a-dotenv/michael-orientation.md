## What is settled

- **The PR #8 mock and its spec scenario go in this change.** Settled by decision 1 (Joel approved the card as described: "also removes the `node:fs` mock") and by the deletion test, which I ran in `scratch-michael/repo` (a `git archive HEAD` copy): with `src/dotenv.ts`, `test/dotenv.test.ts`, `.env.example`, `index.ts:3` and `:10` removed, a poisoned `.env` (`OMLX_MODEL_SETTINGS_PATH` → overlay `{"models":{"real-model":{"max_tokens":0}}}`) gives 93/93 with the mock present **and** 93/93 with the mock removed; `npm run check` exits 0 either way. I first reproduced the archived evidence at HEAD (mock removed + poisoned `.env` → 10 failed/85 passed) so the fixture is the one that bites. After the loader is gone, nothing can make the scenario "A .env beside index.ts does not reach the suite" fail, so by Joel's rule (a test that stays green when the behavior is removed proves nothing) the scenario and the mock go. Consequence the design must carry: a delta `specs/test-isolation/spec.md` with `## MODIFIED Requirements` rewriting "Every Run Isolates Itself From Operator State" without the sentence "It SHALL keep the `.env` beside `index.ts` from reaching the run's environment" and without that scenario (format as in the archived `verification` delta). Code consequence: `worker-setup.ts` loses the `vi.mock` block, `import type { PathLike }` and the `vi` import (biome flags them unused otherwise); the guard, `global-setup.ts` and `CALLER_ENV_KEYS` stay (callers' exports still reach the suite).
- **Behavior change for `.env` users is real, bounded, and already Joel's.** Decision 4 bounds it: only a clone-installed user with no saved `omlx` login and a `.env` beside `index.ts` loses anything; they fall to "Nothing stored and nothing set registers the login-only provider" and `/login omlx` is the documented path. Published installs never carried a `.env` (`package.json` `files` whitelist; measured `npm pack --dry-run` with a `.env` present: 19 files, none of them `.env`, with or without `.npmignore`'s `.env*` line). Joel himself: no `.env` or `.envrc` in `~/Projects/pi-omlx`, a stored `omlx` oauth entry in `auth.json` (key names only read), no `OMLX_*` exported. The card is Joel's request (audit finding 3, must-fix), so the consequence is his decision already; the design states it under consequences and CONFIGURATION.md/README say the `.env` is no longer read (docs pass). One real semantic shift the design must name: the `.env` was global to every pi session; direnv is per-launch-directory. That is the point of the card ("without touching pi"), and ADR 0001 makes `/login` the global mechanism, so nothing is lost that the specs promise.
- **`CALLER_ENV_KEYS` comment** (`test/isolation/global-setup.ts:12-13`: "from the search of index.ts, src/*.ts and .env.example Eleanor Addendum 1 records"). - **`.gitignore` keeps `.env`; `.npmignore` is untouched.** The acceptance criterion is "anything that pointed people at `.env`"; an ignore entry points nobody anywhere, it keeps a secret out of git. Deletion test: remove the line and a leftover `.env` from before this change, or a direnv `.env` (direnv loads `.env` under `load_dotenv`, Chidi F-line 41), becomes committable with a key in it. `.npmignore`'s `.env*` is measured non-load-bearing (above) and the card does not require touching it; leave it.
